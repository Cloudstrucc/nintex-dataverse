Product brief

Electronic Signature Service

Secure, legally binding e-signatures for Elections Canada.

An internal service that lets Elections Canada staff send, sign and track regulated documents electronically — built on Nintex AssureSign and the Elections Canada Microsoft 365 / Dataverse environment, and governed by the Canada Elections Act and GC IT security standards.

Purpose & scope

Replace paper and ad-hoc signing with a single, auditable workflow for consents, appointments, nominations and declarations — without recipients needing an account beyond their Entra ID.

✉

Send & track envelopes

Create signature requests from approved templates and follow every recipient's progress in real time.

▤

Reusable templates

Prepare a document and its signature fields once, then reuse it across your team.

🖹

Signed & sealed documents

Completed documents are sealed and stored with a downloadable, tamper-evident audit trail.

Security & compliance

  • Sign-in with Microsoft Entra ID single sign-on — no separate portal password.
  • Access gated by web roles & table permissions; each user sees only their own records.
  • Every signature event logged with a tamper-evident audit trail (timestamps, IPs, SHA-256 seal).
  • Aligned with the Canada Elections Act, the Privacy Act and GC Protected B / ITSG-33 controls.
  • All transaction state managed in Dataverse within the Elections Canada tenant.

Getting access

The service is internal and invitation-based. Request access, and once approved you sign in with your Elections Canada Entra ID account and land on My Workspace.
