Signing in & access

Process
  1. Go to the portal and select Sign in with Entra ID. You're sent to the Elections Canada sign-in page; if you already have a Microsoft 365 session you're signed in automatically (single sign-on).
  2. Your name and email are taken from your Entra ID profile — nothing to fill in.
  3. You land on My Workspace, where you can create envelopes and track signing status.
Troubleshooting
  • Access denied after sign-in — your account hasn't been granted the e-Sign role yet. Open a support request.
  • Wrong account — sign out of Microsoft 365 in the browser and sign in with your EC account.
