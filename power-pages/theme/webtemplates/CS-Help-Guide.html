Getting started

Overview

The Electronic Signature Service lets Elections Canada teams request legally binding signatures on official documents — no printing, scanning or mailing. You create an envelope from an approved template, add signers, and send it for signature through Nintex AssureSign. Every step is tracked and every completed document carries a tamper-evident audit trail.

Get access & sign in

The portal is private and uses your Elections Canada Entra ID account.

  1. Go to the portal and choose Sign in with Entra ID; use your EC work account.
  2. On first sign-in you are matched to your contact record by email and automatically granted the Authenticated Users role.
  3. If you see “access denied” or a private-site notice, your account has not been added yet — request access from the service team (see Contact support) with your name and EC email.
Access is governed centrally. Elevated actions (managing templates for a whole team) may require an additional role — ask the service team if a template option is greyed out.

Your workspace

My Workspace is your home page. The tiles at the top filter your envelope list by status (Total, In process, Completed, Action required), and the My services cards jump you into Envelopes, Templates, Signed documents and Help. The bell in the header opens your notification centre.

Envelopes

Create an envelope

  1. Open Envelopes and select Create envelope (also available from the list controls and My Workspace).
  2. Choose an approved template, or upload a document to sign.
  3. Give the envelope a name and, optionally, a subject and message for the notification email.

Add signers

For each recipient, enter their full name and email and set their role. You can add several signers; how they are ordered is covered under Signing sequence. Per-signer options (authentication, language) are set here too.

Send & track

Select Send envelope to submit it to Nintex AssureSign. The status moves Draft → Preparing → Ready to send → In process → Completed. Track live status from the envelope list and the workspace tiles; use the full-screen button for a focused view and the page-size control to show more rows.

Cancel an envelope

From the envelope’s ⋮ row menu choose Cancel envelope. Cancellation is only available while the envelope is still active and not yet completed, and cannot be undone — signers can no longer complete it.

Templates

Create a template

Templates are reusable, pre-approved documents with their signer roles and fields already configured, so envelopes are faster and consistent.

  1. Open Templates and select Create template.
  2. Enter a name, description and category so it is easy to find later.
  3. Set Active when it is ready — only active templates appear when creating an envelope.

Upload the document

Attach the source PDF to the template. It is stored with the template and used as the base document each time the template is used; it can be downloaded later from the template’s ⋮ menu.

Use a template

From the Templates list, open a template and choose Use template (or Use in the row menu) to start a new envelope pre-filled from it. You then add the specific signers and send.

Signing setup (Nintex)

Signing sequence

The signing order controls when each recipient receives the request. Nintex AssureSign supports:

  • Sequential — signer 1 must finish before signer 2 is notified, and so on. Use this for approvals that must happen in order.
  • Parallel — all signers are notified at once and can sign in any order. Use this when order does not matter.

Set each signer’s order number when you add them; equal numbers sign in parallel, ascending numbers sign in sequence.

Authentication

Per signer you can require an identity check before they can sign — for example an access code / KBA-style challenge or email-based verification, depending on what your Nintex account allows. Choose the method appropriate to the sensitivity of the document.

Language

Set each signer’s language (English or French) so their signing experience and notification emails are delivered in that language.

Reminders & expiry

Configure automatic reminder emails for signers who have not yet acted, and an expiry (days to complete) after which the envelope lapses. You can also send a manual reminder to a pending signer at any time.

Signature fields & inputs

Templates define where signatures, initials, dates and form inputs appear on the document, and which signer each field belongs to. Configure these in the template so every envelope created from it places fields consistently. Sender inputs let the person creating the envelope supply values (such as a name or amount) that are merged into the document before sending.

Getting the fields and roles right on the template is the single biggest time-saver — it means anyone can create a correct envelope in a few clicks.
After signing

Notifications

The bell in the header shows your unread count and opens the notification centre. Messages are grouped as Action required or For information; filter with the tabs, mark items read, or archive them.

Signed documents

When an envelope completes, the signed PDF and its audit certificate are available under Signed documents. Completed documents are retained per the Elections Canada retention schedule.

Activity & history

Activity & history (under your profile menu) shows a combined, filterable record of your envelopes, templates, documents and notifications.

Support

Frequently asked questions

A signer didn’t get the email — what do I check?

Confirm the email address on the signer, ask them to check junk mail, and send a manual reminder. For sequential signing, earlier signers must finish first.

Can I change an envelope after sending?

No. Once sent, an envelope can only be cancelled and recreated. Review recipients carefully before sending.

Why can’t I see a template when creating an envelope?

Only Active templates appear. Open the template and set it active, or ask the owner to.

Who can manage templates?

Any authenticated user can create templates; some team-wide options may require an elevated role — see Contact support.

Contact support

Still stuck? Reach the Electronic Signature support team and include the envelope or template name and what you were doing.
