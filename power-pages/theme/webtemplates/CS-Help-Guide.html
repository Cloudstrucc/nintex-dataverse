{{ snippets['ESIGN/HELP/Q1'] | default: 'Getting started' }}

Sign in with your Entra ID. Your workspace shows your envelopes, quick services and any notifications. Use My services to jump into Envelopes, Templates or Signed documents.

{{ snippets['ESIGN/HELP/Q2'] | default: 'Create and send an envelope' }}
  1. Open Envelopes and choose Create a new envelope.
  2. Pick an approved template or upload a document.
  3. Add signers, set the signing order, authentication and language.
  4. Review and send — signers are notified by email.
{{ snippets['ESIGN/HELP/Q3'] | default: 'Track signing progress' }}

The envelope table on My Workspace shows live status. Filter by In process, Completed or Action required using the tiles. Reminders can be sent to pending signers.

{{ snippets['ESIGN/HELP/Q4'] | default: 'Retrieve signed documents' }}

When an envelope completes, its signed PDF and audit trail are available under Signed documents. Completed documents are retained per the Elections Canada retention schedule.

{{ snippets['ESIGN/HELP/Q5'] | default: 'Templates' }}

Templates are reusable, pre-approved documents. Browse existing templates or create a new one from Templates. Only active templates appear when creating an envelope.
